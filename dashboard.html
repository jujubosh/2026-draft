Cheat sheet — ranked by value over replacement

VORP = projected PPR points above the last startable player at the position (QB14 / RB34 / WR36 / TE14) — how much a player actually wins you over the guy you'd stream. Value = ADP rank minus VORP rank: green means drafts are taking him later than his projection deserves. Depth = ESPN depth chart (S = listed starter, 2/3 = backup). ADP = average draft position on Sleeper. ECR = FantasyPros 86-expert consensus rank — a second opinion; when ECR and ADP disagree hard, someone is wrong about him. Rec = projected receptions (the stat full PPR pays for).

Method. Player pool: Sleeper 2026 season projections (full PPR) and PPR ADP. Each simulated draft gives every opponent a private board of ADP plus noise that grows with ADP (top picks barely move; round-10 players swing by rounds), with roster-need logic and late K/DST. Your team follows the strategy script for the early rounds, then best VORP with need urgency. Team score = optimal lineup (QB, 2RB, 2WR, TE, flex, K, DST) projected points plus a 12% credit on the top three bench players. Each simulated season then re-samples every player's actual output with position-sized error (RB ±34%, WR ±30%, TE ±32%, QB ±22%, lognormal) — league rank, floor, and ceiling come from those sampled outcomes, so strategy risk reflects that early RBs bust harder than early WRs.

Strategies define rounds 1–4 only (* = best value). Hero RB — one elite RB, then three straight WRs — wins nearly every slot in full PPR: the round-1 RB clears replacement by 100+ points, while rounds 2–4 are the reception-heavy WR sweet spot and startable pass-catching RBs (Swift, Skattebo) are still on the board in round 5.
